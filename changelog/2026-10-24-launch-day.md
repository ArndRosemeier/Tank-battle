# Version numbers, and everything built so far
- The game now shows its version, and this What's new screen lists every change.
- TRON look: much more glow, light trails and a neon arena.
- Rematch button after victory or defeat, and a custom battle setup screen (teams, tanks per team, time limit).
- Triangle landscapes: hills, caves and overhangs that you can blast apart, with loose rock collapsing.
- Online play: create or join a room and battle friends over the internet.
- Arena generator: pick a seed and a size and get a fresh, fair arena every time.
- Launched from cued.de, the game shows who is playing and uses that name online.
- Fixed a crash in online matches when your tank had already been destroyed.
