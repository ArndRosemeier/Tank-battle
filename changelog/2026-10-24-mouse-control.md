# Mouse control
- The mouse now turns the view: look and shoot in any direction while you drive with WASD.
- Three mouse modes in Settings: Edge push (default), Centre aim and Free edge. Your choice is saved.
- Edge push: the game captures the mouse; push the crosshair against a screen edge to turn. Works on multi-monitor setups.
- Centre aim: crosshair in the middle, the mouse turns and tilts the view.
- Free edge: normal cursor; rest it near the left or right edge to turn.
- New Mouse speed slider. Press C to snap the view back behind your tank. Esc gives the mouse back and pauses.
